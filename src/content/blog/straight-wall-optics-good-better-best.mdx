## How to Pick a Straight-Wall Scope

Good/Better/Best isn't about spending more for the sake of it — it's about what you're trading up for at each step:

- **Good** gets you dependable glass that does the core job: hold zero, handle the cartridge's recoil, and give you a usable sight picture out to 250 yards.
- **Better** adds one real feature that changes how you hunt — illumination, wider cartridge calibration, or stronger low-light performance.
- **Best** is built around your exact cartridge and load, cutting out reticle guesswork almost entirely.

None of these are the wrong pick. A Good-tier scope on a well-zeroed rifle will fill a tag just as fast as a Best-tier one — the tiers above are about how much guesswork you want to remove from the shot, not a floor on whether the gear will work. If you're still deciding between a BDC and a plain duplex reticle in the first place, see [BDC vs. Duplex: How to Choose a Reticle for Straight-Wall Rifles](/blog/reticle-choice-bdc-vs-duplex) for the full breakdown.
